Data Deletion Request

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This page explains how to have your data deleted from apps published by Insiders Digital Pty Ltd (ACN 698 801 151), including Snowhaven Inn and Dark Verdict.

Most of it is already in your hands

Our apps have no user accounts. There is no registration, no login, no password and no email address attached to you — so there is no account to delete. Almost everything our apps hold about you is stored on your own device.

Deleting the app from your device permanently deletes your save file — your progress, your settings and everything you have unlocked. This cannot be undone, and we cannot recover it for you.

What we hold on our side

A small amount of anonymous information is held away from your device. In every case it is identified only by a randomly generated identifier created on your device, never by your name, email or advertising identifier.

Snowhaven Inn

  • anonymous records of which screens were opened and which actions were taken;
  • crash reports, including device model, operating system version and app version.

Dark Verdict

  • a record of each daily case your device has played — the case, the suspect you accused, whether you were right, and when;
  • if you turned notifications on, the push token for your device and whether notifications are enabled.

Crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days, and analytics records after 12 months. The others remain until you ask us to remove them, or until the app is no longer published.

How to request deletion

Email us and we will delete everything we hold that is associated with your device:

hello@insidersdigital.com.au

Please include:

  1. the name of the app — Snowhaven Inn or Dark Verdict;
  2. the anonymous identifier shown in the app's Settings screen, if you still have the app installed. This is the only way we can locate records belonging to your device, because we hold nothing else that identifies you.

What happens next

We aim to acknowledge your request within 7 days and to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days, subject to any information we are legally required to retain. We will confirm by email when it is done.

If you no longer have the app installed and cannot provide the anonymous identifier, we may be unable to locate any records relating to you. Where we cannot identify you from the data we hold, we are not required to collect additional information in order to do so — and in practice, the records will expire on their own within 12 months.
