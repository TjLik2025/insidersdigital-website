Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all apps and services developed and distributed by Insiders Digital Pty Ltd (ACN 698 801 151), Sydney, NSW, Australia, including Dark Verdict and Snowhaven Inn. By using our apps, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

We collect as little as our apps can function with. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not know your name. Section 6 sets out what each individual app collects, because they differ.

1. Information We Collect

Across our apps we may collect:

  • Your progress in the app — what you have completed, unlocked or saved. In most cases this never leaves your device.
  • Anonymous device identifiers — randomly generated values that let us count how many people use a feature, or recognise a purchase after you reinstall. They are not your device's advertising identifier and cannot be used to identify you personally.
  • Crash and error reports — a technical description of a fault, including the type of device, the operating system version and the app version, so that we can fix it.
  • How features are used — anonymous records of which screens are opened and which actions are taken, so we can see where people get stuck.
  • Push notification tokens — where an app sends notifications, an anonymous token identifying your device so that we can deliver them. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Purchase information — where an app offers purchases, transactions are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

None of our apps offer user accounts. There is no registration, no login, no password and no cloud sync in any app we publish.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, and have never collected:

  • Your name, postal address or phone number
  • Your email address, unless you choose to email us for support
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Your contacts, photos, camera or microphone
  • Your device's advertising identifier
  • Any information used to build an advertising or marketing profile about you
  • Any special category or sensitive information, including health information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers.

4. How Long We Keep It

  • On-device data stays until you delete the app.
  • Crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Analytics events are retained for up to 12 months and then deleted.
  • Purchase records are kept as long as the law requires us to keep financial records.
  • Support correspondence is retained only as long as necessary to resolve your issue.

5. Security

All data our apps send is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Because we hold no accounts, no passwords and no contact details, there is no login to compromise.

6. What Each App Collects

Snowhaven Inn

No account is required. Your save file — your progress, the rooms you have restored, the notes you have found and your settings — is stored only on your device. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it if you delete the app.

The app sends us:

  • anonymous records of how features are used, through PostHog, identified only by a random identifier generated on your device;
  • crash and error reports, through Sentry;
  • its version and platform when checking for an update.

The app does not contain advertising, does not use an advertising identifier, and currently contains no in-app purchases. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the change is released.

Dark Verdict

Dark Verdict has no user accounts. There is no registration, no login and no cloud sync. Your game progress — streak, rank, accusation history and settings — is stored only on your device.

Two things are held on our servers, both identified only by a randomly generated device identifier created on your device, and neither linked to your name, email or advertising identifier:

  • a record of each daily case your device has played — the case, the suspect you accused, whether you were right, and when — so that the daily case works correctly and cannot be replayed;
  • if you turn notifications on, the push token for your device and whether notifications are enabled, so that we can deliver them.

Subscriptions, including Dark Verdict Pro, are processed by Apple or Google and verified through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier so that your subscription survives a reinstall.

Crash reports are provided only if you have opted in to sharing analytics with Apple or Google.

7. Deleting Your Data

Because we hold no accounts, most of your data is yours to delete directly: deleting the app from your device removes your save file and all on-device data permanently. We cannot recover it, and neither can you.

For the anonymous server-side records described above — analytics and crash reports for Snowhaven Inn, solve records and push tokens for Dark Verdict — you can ask us to delete anything associated with your device. See our Data Deletion Request page for how to do that and what to expect.

Because none of our apps have accounts, there is no account to close. Deleting the app and asking us to remove the device records leaves us holding nothing about you at all.

8. Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights (European Union / United Kingdom)

If you are in the EU or UK, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, to object to processing, and to request that we transfer it to another provider. Our lawful basis for processing crash reports and anonymous usage data is our legitimate interest in keeping our apps working and improving them.

Because we do not hold an account for you, we may need you to provide the anonymous identifier shown in the app's settings before we can locate any data about you. If we cannot identify you from the data we hold, we are not required to collect additional information in order to do so.

You may lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

10. Your Rights (Australia — Privacy Act 1988)

The Australian Privacy Principles give you the right to ask what personal information we hold about you and to ask us to correct it. Contact us at the address below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
