Snowhaven Inn Dark Verdict

Snowhaven Inn

Where is my progress saved? Will I lose the inn if I delete the app?
Your inn is saved on your device only. There is no account and no cloud backup, which means deleting the app permanently deletes your progress — we cannot recover it, and neither can you. Reinstalling starts a new inn from the beginning. Updating the app is completely safe; only deleting it removes your save.
Do I need an account to play?
No. There is no sign-up, no login and no password. Open the app and play.
I've run out of energy.
Energy refills on its own over time, including while the app is closed, so come back a little later and you'll have more. You don't need to leave the app open for it to refill.
My board is full and I can't merge anything.
Merge matching items together to combine them into a single better item — that's how you free up space. Delivering finished items to the orders at the top of the board also clears room. If you're completely stuck, merging the smallest items first usually opens the board back up.
How do the lanterns work?
There are thirty-one windows at Snowhaven, and one lantern for each. You hang them from the Lantern Ledger as you restore the inn and work through the chapters. The Ledger shows you how many are lit and what you'll need for the next one.
Can I replay a chapter, or watch a room's reveal again?
Not at the moment. Chapters move forward only, and each room's reveal plays once when you restore it. The same is true of the letters you find — they belong to the story as it unfolds, and can't be re-opened afterwards. The keepsakes you collect are kept in the Album, so you can revisit those whenever you like.
Can I play without an internet connection?
Yes. Merging, restoring rooms and the story all work offline. You'll need a connection for live events to update and to receive app updates.
How do I turn the music or sound off?
Open Settings from the Home screen and turn the audio off there. The setting is remembered between sessions.
Does Snowhaven Inn have ads or in-app purchases?
No. There is no advertising and there are no in-app purchases in the current version. If that ever changes, we'll say so clearly in the release notes and update our Privacy Policy before the change ships.
The app crashed, or something didn't load properly.
Force-close the app and open it again — that resolves most one-off problems, and your progress is safe. Please don't delete and reinstall, as that erases your inn. If the problem keeps happening, email us with your device model, your operating system version and what you were doing at the time, and we'll look into it.

Dark Verdict

When does a new case drop?
A new mystery is generated every day at midnight (your local time). Open the app after midnight to find today's fresh case waiting for you.
I missed yesterday's case. Can I still play it?
Past cases are available in the Archive, which is unlocked with a Dark Verdict Pro subscription. Free players can only access today's case.
I made the wrong accusation. Can I try again?
No — each case allows only one accusation. This is by design. The one-accusation rule is what makes each case meaningful. Think carefully before you accuse!
My streak reset even though I played yesterday.
Streaks require a completed accusation (not just opening the case). If you read the case file but didn't make an accusation, the streak won't count. Make sure to complete each case fully before midnight.
Where is my progress saved?
On your device. There is no account and no cloud backup, so deleting the app permanently deletes your streak, rank and history. Updating the app is safe; only deleting it removes your progress.
How do I cancel my Dark Verdict Pro subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel: open Settings on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → find Dark Verdict → tap Cancel Subscription. Your Pro access continues until the end of the current billing period.
I was charged but I don't have Pro access.
Try tapping "Restore Purchases" in the app settings. If the issue persists, contact us at the email below with your Apple receipt and we'll resolve it promptly.
The app is crashing or not loading a case.
Force-close the app and open it again. If the problem persists, email us with your device model and iOS version. Please don't delete and reinstall unless we ask you to, as that erases your progress.
How do I request a refund?
Refunds are processed by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund for any App Store purchase.
